Builders

Built for people who hate soft systems.

If you are tired of apps that pretend the domain is simple, or of agents that invent behavior at runtime, you are in the right place.

Founders designing a real domain

You know the entities, the edge cases, the permissions that matter. You do not want to wait a year for a custom CRUD stack that freezes those decisions into the wrong layer. bsmiq lets you declare the business and operate it while you learn.

Platform and internal systems teams

You already maintain auth, data stores, and integrations. What you lack is a shared, executable model of the business that product and ops can evolve without inventing a new microservice for every noun.

AI-native builders

You want Claude (or any strong model) to draft modules, rules, and entity shapes — then compile those drafts into something that either runs correctly or fails loudly. bsmiq is the compile target and the runtime contract.

What you should expect

  • Precision over magic Diagnostics with codes. Refused commands. Explicit versions.
  • Model first UI and agents help author the specification; they do not replace it.
  • Room to go deep Sub-entities, relationships, projections, workflows, login providers — the engine is meant to absorb seriousness.

What this is not

  • Not a prompt playground Chat is optional. Correctness is not.
  • Not another form builder If your domain is only screens, you do not need bsmiq.
  • Not vapor We are building the engine and the operating UI in the same breath as the thesis.

Talk to us

Especially if you are evaluating AI × systems partnerships — Claude startup program, pilots, or a domain you want to model end-to-end.
