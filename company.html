Company

A small company, a sharp problem.

bsmiq exists to collapse the distance between how a business is described and how its software behaves — especially as AI starts writing more of both.

Mission

Make the business specification the runnable system: precise enough for an engine, clear enough for founders and operators, structured enough for AI to help improve it safely.

Stage

Early. Building the engine, the authoring interface, and the operating surfaces in the open daylight of real product use — not as a slide deck about a future platform.
